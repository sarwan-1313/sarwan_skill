- **Cause:** the regex in `tinyparse/size.py` matched the digits after the decimal point but didn't capture them, so `1.5k` was read as `1k`.
- **Fix:** the regex now captures the whole number, and `parse_size` multiplies it with `Fraction` so the result is exact. The result is truncated to an int.
