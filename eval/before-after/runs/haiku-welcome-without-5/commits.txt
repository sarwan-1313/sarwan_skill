Fix parse_size dropping the fractional part

parse_size("1.5k") returned 1024 because the fractional digits were
matched but never captured, and the integer part alone was multiplied.
Capture the full decimal and multiply it exactly with Decimal, so
"1.5k" gives 1536. Results are still truncated to whole bytes.
