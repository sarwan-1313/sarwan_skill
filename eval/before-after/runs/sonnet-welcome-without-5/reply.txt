`parse_size("1.5k")` now returns 1536. The fix is committed on a new branch, `fix-parse-size-decimals`, and the full suite passes (8 tests). Nothing is pushed and no pull request is open.

- **Cause:** the regex in `tinyparse/size.py` matched the `.5` in `1.5k` but didn't capture it, so only the `1` was used.
- **Fix:** the regex now captures the whole number, and the multiplication uses `fractions.Fraction` so there's no float rounding. The result is truncated to an int, and whole numbers behave as before.
- **Test:** I added `test_decimals` in `tests/test_size.py`. It covers `1.5k`, `1.5M`, `0.5 KiB` and `2.k`.
