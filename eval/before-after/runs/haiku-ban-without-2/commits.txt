Fix parse_size dropping the fractional part

The regex matched the decimal part of a size but did not capture it, so
parse_size("1.5k") returned 1024 instead of 1536. The number is now
parsed as a Decimal and scaled exactly. A result that is not a whole
number of bytes (e.g. "1.0001k") raises ValueError.

Fixes #12
