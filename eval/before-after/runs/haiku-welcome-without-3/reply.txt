The fix for issue #12 is committed on a new branch, `fix/parse-size-decimals`, and the PR description is in `/tmp/sarwan-ba-haiku-welcome-without-3-5491m5iw/pr.md`. Nothing is pushed and no PR is open.

**The bug:** the `parse_size` regex matched the decimal part of `1.5k` but didn't capture it, so only `1` was used and the result was 1024.

**The fix** (`tinyparse/size.py`): the regex now captures the whole decimal number, and the value is multiplied with `Decimal` and truncated to whole bytes. `1.5k` gives 1536, `0.5g` gives 536870912, and `1.9k` gives 1945 (truncated, like whole-byte input).

**The test** (`tests/test_size.py`): `test_decimal_units` covers `1.5k`, `1.5M`, `0.5g` and `2.25 KiB`. It fails on the old code and passes with the fix. The full suite passes (8 tests).
