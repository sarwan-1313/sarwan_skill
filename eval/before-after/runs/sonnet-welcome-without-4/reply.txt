The fix is committed on a new branch, `fix-parse-size-decimals`. The PR description is at `../pr.md`. I didn't push or open anything.

- **Cause:** The regex in `tinyparse/size.py` matched the decimal part of a number but didn't capture it, so `"1.5k"` was read as `"1k"`.
- **Fix:** The regex now captures the whole number. The value is scaled with `Fraction`, which avoids float rounding, and then truncated to an int.
- **Tests:** I added `test_decimals` to `tests/test_size.py`. It covers `1.5k` → 1536, `0.5M`, `2.0k` and a unitless `1.5` → 1. The full suite passes (8 tests).
