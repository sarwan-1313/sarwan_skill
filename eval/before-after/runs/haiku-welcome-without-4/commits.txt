Fix parse_size dropping the fractional part

The regex matched the integer digits of "1.5k" as "1" and discarded
the ".5", so parse_size("1.5k") returned 1024 instead of 1536.
Capture the full decimal number and compute with Decimal so the
result is exact before truncating to an int.

Fixes #12
