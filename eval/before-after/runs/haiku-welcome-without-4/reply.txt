**The bug:** the regex in `tinyparse/size.py` matched the integer digits of `1.5k` as `1` and discarded the `.5`, so the result was 1024.

**The fix:** the regex now captures the full decimal number, and the value is computed with `Decimal` before truncating to `int`. `1.5k` gives 1536. Whole numbers behave as before.
