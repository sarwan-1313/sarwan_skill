**The bug:** the regex in `tinyparse/size.py` matched the decimal part of a size but didn't capture it, so `int("1.5")` silently dropped the `.5`. I reproduced it: `parse_size("1.5k")` returned `1024`.

**The fix:** the regex now captures the full number, and the value is multiplied as a `Decimal` so fractions stay exact, then truncated to whole bytes. `"1.5k"` now returns `1536`, and whole numbers like `"2k"` are unchanged.

**Tests:** I added `test_decimal_units` covering `"1.5k"`, `"0.5k"`, `"1.5 MiB"` and `"2.25G"`. It fails on the old code and passes with the fix. The full suite passes (8 tests).
