Fix parse_size dropping the fraction of decimal sizes

The regex matched the fractional part of a size but did not capture it,
so int("1.5") discarded the ".5" and parse_size("1.5k") returned 1024
instead of 1536. Capture the full number and multiply it as a Decimal
so fractions stay exact, then truncate to a whole number of bytes.
